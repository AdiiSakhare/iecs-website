Our Story

IECS - Industrial Engineering Control Services - was founded by an electrical engineer with over a decade of hands-on experience across manufacturing, automation and plant maintenance.

Instead of starting as a trading or reselling business, IECS was built around solving real problems on the factory floor: control panels that kept failing, conveyors that needed custom guarding, workstations that slowed operators down. That practical, engineering-first approach is still how every project starts today.

We work directly with manufacturers as well as distributors and channel partners, taking projects from concept and control-panel design through fabrication, installation and long-term maintenance.
